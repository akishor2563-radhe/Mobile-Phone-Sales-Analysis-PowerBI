# 📱 Mobile Phone Sales Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## 🚀 Project Overview
This Power BI project delivers a comprehensive analysis of mobile phone sales performance. The dashboard evaluates global and regional sales trends, brand performance, unit distribution, and revenue generation to support strategic business decisions.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Advanced data modeling, interactive dashboards, DAX measures, and visual storytelling.
* **Excel / Power Query:** Data cleaning, transformation, and multi-source integration.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Sales Revenue:** Comprehensive tracking of mobile brand earnings.
* **Units Sold:** Volume-based performance metrics across models and regions.
* **Brand Contribution:** Market share and revenue breakdown by top mobile brands.

## 📈 Dashboard Features & Visuals
* **Brand Performance Visuals:** Comparative analysis of leading smartphone manufacturers.
* **Regional Sales Distribution:** Geographic mapping and territory breakdowns.
* **Trend Analysis:** Time-series charts tracking monthly and quarterly sales growth.

## 💡 Business Insights
* Identification of top-performing smartphone brands and models.
* Clear visibility into peak sales seasons and high-demand regions.
* Actionable data to optimize inventory and marketing strategies.
